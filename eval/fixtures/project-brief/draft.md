# Project Brief: Strategic Realignment of Our Small-Clinic Customer Segment

## Introduction

As Tidewell continues to grow and mature, it is essential that we periodically reassess our customer segments to ensure we are delivering maximum value while building a sustainable, scalable business. This brief outlines a strategic initiative to realign our approach to clinics with fewer than three dentists.

## Context

Our analysis has surfaced an important insight. Clinics under 3 dentists account for 61% of our churn, yet they represent only 18% of our revenue. Notably, these clinics pay the same $399 per month as our larger customers and cost us the same amount to support. This imbalance underscores the need for a more focused go-to-market strategy.

## Objectives

The primary objective of this initiative is to sharpen our focus on the customers for whom Tidewell delivers the greatest value. By doing so, we aim to reduce churn, improve unit economics, and strengthen our position in the market.

## Proposed Approach

We propose a thoughtful, phased approach. Effective immediately, we will stop new sales to clinics with fewer than three dentists. Existing small clinics will continue to receive full service for 60 days, after which we will offer them a referral to a more affordable tool better suited to their needs. Sales will stop pitching these clinics this week.

## Ownership

The Head of Customer Success will own this initiative, working closely with Sales and Support to ensure a smooth and respectful transition for affected customers.

## Success Criteria

This project will be considered complete when no small clinic remains on an active plan after May 1, as confirmed by our churn report.

## Risks and Considerations

It's important to acknowledge that some small clinics are vocal on review sites, and there is a risk of negative feedback. We will approach every conversation with empathy and transparency to mitigate this risk.

## Conclusion

Overall, this initiative represents a critical step in Tidewell's evolution. By focusing our resources where they matter most, we position ourselves for long-term success.
