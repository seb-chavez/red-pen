# Weekly Status Update: Two-Way SMS Confirmations

Hi team,

I hope everyone had a great week! I wanted to take a moment to share a comprehensive update on the progress of our Two-Way SMS Confirmations initiative, which continues to be a key priority for Tidewell.

**Highlights**

We are thrilled to report some very encouraging early results from our pilot clinics. In the first week, the confirmed-appointment rate increased by 31%. This is a powerful early signal that the feature is resonating with patients and delivering real value to practices.

**Challenges**

As with any ambitious initiative, we have encountered some challenges along the way. Most notably, our carrier 10DLC registration is still pending. Until it is approved, we are unable to send messages at full volume. The team is actively working with the carrier to resolve this as quickly as possible.

**Timeline**

Given the registration dependency, there is a possibility that our launch date may shift. If the registration is not approved by Friday, launch will move from May 5 to May 12.

**Asks**

To keep things moving, we need Legal to review and sign off on the SMS consent wording by Wednesday.

**Next Steps**

We will continue to monitor pilot results closely, follow up with the carrier, and keep everyone informed of any developments.

Thank you all for your continued hard work and dedication. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or feedback!

Best,
The SMS Team
