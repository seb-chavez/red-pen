Tidewell founder brief notes

- we sell scheduling + no-show reduction software to independent dental practices
- target: practices with 3 to 15 dentists, owner-run, no in-house IT
- ARR $1.2M, 61 clinics, monthly churn 4.1% (too high, mostly clinics under 3 dentists)
- price $399/month per clinic flat
- problem: front-desk staff spend ~2 hrs/day on reminder calls; no-shows cost an avg clinic ~$9k/month
- why now: big PMS vendors raised prices 30% this year, clinics shopping around
- competitor ChairSync: bigger, sells to DSOs (chains), complex setup ~6 weeks; we set up in 1 day
- our proof: pilot clinics cut no-shows 38% over 90 days
- biggest risk: churn. if we can't get churn under 2% we don't have a business
- plan: stop selling to clinics under 3 dentists, add two-way SMS confirmations
- kill condition: if fewer than 12 paying clinics in the 3-15 segment renew by March 31, rethink segment
- team: 2 founders (ex dental software + ex Stripe), 3 eng
