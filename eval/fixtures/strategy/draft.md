# Tidewell: Strategic Vision and Founder Brief

## Executive Summary

In today's rapidly evolving healthcare landscape, independent dental practices face unprecedented challenges. Tidewell is positioned at the intersection of patient engagement and practice operations, offering a transformative, seamless platform that empowers dental teams to focus on what truly matters: delivering exceptional patient care. This document outlines our strategic vision, our market opportunity, and the pivotal decisions that will shape our journey forward.

## Our Mission

Our mission is not simply to build scheduling software. It's to fundamentally reimagine how dental practices connect with their patients, fostering stronger relationships, reducing operational friction, and unlocking new levels of efficiency across the entire practice ecosystem. We believe that every practice, regardless of size, deserves access to world-class tools that were once reserved only for large enterprises.

## The Problem

It is widely recognized across the industry that missed appointments represent one of the most significant challenges facing dental practices today. Experts agree that no-shows have a profound impact on practice revenue, staff morale, and patient outcomes. For an average clinic, no-shows cost approximately $9,000 every month, a figure that underscores the critical importance of addressing this issue.

Furthermore, front-desk staff spend roughly two hours each day on reminder calls. This is time that could be better spent on higher-value activities, such as greeting patients, coordinating care, and supporting the clinical team. The cumulative effect of this burden cannot be overstated.

## Why Now

The timing has never been better. This year, the major practice management system vendors raised their prices by 30%, and as a result, clinics are actively shopping around for alternatives. This represents a unique and time-sensitive window of opportunity for Tidewell to capture market share and establish itself as a trusted partner.

## Our Target Customer

We focus on owner-run independent dental practices with 3 to 15 dentists and no in-house IT. These practices are large enough to feel the pain of no-shows acutely, yet small enough that they are underserved by enterprise solutions. They value simplicity, speed, and a vendor who truly understands their unique needs.

## Traction

We are proud to share that Tidewell has achieved meaningful traction. We currently serve 61 clinics and have reached $1.2M in annual recurring revenue. Our pricing is a simple, transparent flat fee of $399 per month per clinic, which our customers appreciate. Most importantly, our pilot clinics reduced no-shows by 38% over 90 days, a powerful testament to the value of our platform.

## Competitive Landscape

The competitive landscape is dynamic. ChairSync is the most notable competitor. While ChairSync is a larger company, it primarily serves dental service organizations (DSOs), and its implementation is complex, typically requiring around six weeks. In contrast, Tidewell can be set up in a single day. This is not just a feature; it's a fundamental difference in philosophy.

## Challenges and Risks

Like any growing company, we face challenges. Our monthly churn is currently 4.1%, which is higher than we would like. Upon analysis, the majority of this churn comes from clinics with fewer than three dentists. Churn is, without question, our biggest risk. If we cannot bring churn below 2%, we do not have a sustainable business.

## Strategic Priorities

Going forward, we will focus on two key priorities. First, we will stop selling to clinics with fewer than three dentists. Second, we will add two-way SMS confirmations, enhancing the patient experience and further reducing no-shows.

## Decision Criteria

To hold ourselves accountable, we have established a clear decision point. If fewer than 12 paying clinics in the 3 to 15 dentist segment renew by March 31, we will rethink our target segment.

## Our Team

Our founding team brings deep and complementary expertise. One founder previously built dental software, and the other was at Stripe. We are supported by three talented engineers who share our passion for this mission.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, Tidewell is uniquely positioned to lead the next chapter of dental practice operations. With a clear focus, a differentiated product, and a passionate team, we are confident in our ability to deliver lasting value to dental practices and their patients. We look forward to the journey ahead.
